03. LLM_REASONING_AUDITS_&_VECTOR_GEOMETRY

Spot the AI Reasoning Flaw & Cosine Similarity Gate

Audit Two LLM Proofs (Topological Reasoning)

Prompt: "How many connected components does the centralizer group G of a 4×4 matrix with minimal polynomial m(t) = t³ - t have?"

Trace A: Eigenvalues {0, 1, -1} give eigenspaces of dims (2, 1, 1), so G ≅ GL₂(ℝ) × GL₁(ℝ) × GL₁(ℝ). Since general linear groups are connected, G has 1 component.
Trace B: Eigenvalues {0, 1, -1} split ℝ⁴ into (2, 1, 1) blocks ⇒ G ≅ GL₂(ℝ) × GL₁(ℝ) × GL₁(ℝ). Each GL_k(ℝ) splits into det > 0 and det < 0 (2 components), giving 2³ = 8 components.
