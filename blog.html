How We Scaled Unstructured Speech & Text Training Data by 6,000x

During my ML Engineering internship at Swecha building "Swecha Voice", our primary bottleneck wasn't model architecture—it was severe data scarcity and inconsistent text formatting across raw regional corpora.

Raw Unstructured Text ──▶ Unicode & Regex Normalization ──▶ Automated Deduplication ──▶ Indexed Training Corpus (6,000x Scale)

By replacing manual cleaning with deterministic Python ingestion pipelines, automated schema validation, and batch tokenization, we expanded the clean training database by over 6,000x while cutting preprocessing latency for downstream acoustic and language models.

CASE STUDY 02 • TIME-SERIES & STATISTICS • 5 MIN READ

Why SARIMA Outperformed ARIMA by 60% RMSE on 15-Year Energy Demand

Standard autoregressive models often fail on multi-year utility datasets because power consumption exhibits strong 12-month seasonal cycles alongside long-term economic drift.

SARIMA(1, 1, 1) × (1, 1, 1, 12) ──▶ RMSE: 164.7 MWh | MAPE: 2.38% (vs. AR RMSE: 412.8 MWh)

Using Augmented Dickey-Fuller (ADF) stationarity testing and seasonal differencing ($D=1, s=12$), SARIMA captured recurring summer/winter load peaks that non-seasonal ARIMA smoothed over—reducing out-of-sample RMSE from 412.8 to 164.7.
