INTERACTIVE LLM AUDIT

Spot the AI Reasoning Flaw

Problem: Let $A \in M_4(\mathbb{R})$ have minimal polynomial $m(t) = t^3 - t$. How many connected components can $G = \{X \in GL_4(\mathbb{R}) \mid AX = XA\}$ have?

Model Trace A: Eigenvalues $\{0, 1, -1\}$ have multiplicities $\{2,1,1\}$, so $G \cong GL_2(\mathbb{R}) \times GL_1(\mathbb{R}) \times GL_1(\mathbb{R})$. Since each $GL_n(\mathbb{R})$ is connected, $G$ has 1 component.
Model Trace B: $G \cong GL_2(\mathbb{R}) \times GL_1(\mathbb{R}) \times GL_1(\mathbb{R})$. Each $GL_n(\mathbb{R})$ splits into $\det > 0$ and $\det < 0$ (2 components), giving $2 \times 2 \times 2 = \mathbf{8}$ components.
